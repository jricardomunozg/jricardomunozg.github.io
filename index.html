01 · About

I study how complex systems evolve, stabilize, and can be controlled.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Mathematical Engineering, Universidad de Chile, and principal researcher of the FONDECYT 2026 project Control and Long-Time Behavior for Dispersive Equations, supervised by Prof. Claudio Muñoz.

My work lies at the intersection of partial differential equations, control theory, numerical analysis, and scientific machine learning—with particular interest in dispersive models, Lyapunov equations, and reduced-order methods.

02 · Research

Research directions

Analytical foundations meet computational methods.

01

Control & Stabilization

Boundary and internal control mechanisms for dispersive PDEs, including KP, KdV–KdV, and related systems.

02

Long-Time Dynamics

Energy decay, asymptotic behavior, critical domains, and time-delay effects in nonlinear evolution equations.

03

Operator Equations

Spectral properties and Schatten-class estimates for Lyapunov Gramians in infinite-dimensional control systems.

04

Scientific Machine Learning

Reduced-order models and neural surrogates for parametric optimal control and PDE-constrained optimization.
